I switched to protonmail.

This is one of a huge movement. I've been trying out both gmail, protonmail, and hey for several months now and I found that there are almost no speed difference between gmail with third-party email client (in my case, Spark) and gmail forwarded to protonmail.

In recent months, I tried using iCloud's email alias feature for new services I register. So I switched my iCloud account email to protonmail. Making all existing aliases forwarded to protonmail.

There are tons of small issues in their official iOS app, but it is open-source. So hopefully I can fix it.
