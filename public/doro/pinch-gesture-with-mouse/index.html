pinch gesture with mouse

2025-09-26

MacOS GUI app that allows you to do magic trackpad's 'pinch gesture' with mouse. Works similar to how pinch gesture on iPhone Simulator work.

  1. You left-press somewhere whiile pressing a key.

  2. While holding mouse-left button, drag diagonally
