youtube

2025-05-22

Thoughts

I think YouTube is quite fair platform. It's moderation has some issues, but at YouTube's scale, I think it's pretty decent.

I think current YouTube premium price (10,450won) is reasonable and even quite cheap. Think about it. Many people spend most of their time watching YouTube. YouTube is clearly the biggest entertainment platform. Paying just 10,450won to a service that is making my life a lot more enjoyable is reasonable deal.

BUT

It's still centralized and the box might break or change sometime. So having decentralized alternative would be a good thing.

Real Problem

We search for websites or videos. But youtube is the platform that holds literally every videos in the whole internet. Therefore, it's pretty hard to get out from youtube.

SNS - don't have problem because people don't care much about old posts Web Search - don't have problem because all websites are sort of federated
